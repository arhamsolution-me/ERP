# Sales Management

This directory contains sales management resources, documentation, and module assets for NexERP.

## Features & Sub-modules:
- **POS Terminal (Checkout & Offline Sync)**: Instant barcode lookup, touch product tiles, hold sale, and local IndexedDB transaction queue.
- **POS Cart & Payment**: Split payment methods (Cash, Card, JazzCash, Easypaisa, Credit), automatic change calculator, and instant receipt generation.
- **Transaction History & Refunds**: Searchable transaction ledger, receipt reprints, and supervisor PIN-protected refund workflows.
- **Customer CRM**: Retail and wholesale customer profiles, credit limit monitoring, and purchase histories.
- **Wholesale Order Lifecycle**: Multi-stage orders (`Draft` -> `Confirmed` -> `Fulfilled` -> `Invoiced`), real-time stock reservation, and automated dispatch.
- **Analytics & Branch Comparisons**: Revenue trends, top-selling items, and cross-branch comparative metrics.
